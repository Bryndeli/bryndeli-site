FAQ

Questions carers ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Families and employers pay us when they hire. Carers never pay anything.

Do I need a DBS check?

Most care roles need one. If yours isn't current, we'll explain how to get or update it before any introduction.

Can you sponsor my visa?

We can't sponsor visas ourselves. If an employer we introduce you to can offer sponsorship, we'll tell you clearly before you apply.

Where do you work?

We're starting in Telford and Shropshire. Join from anywhere and we'll let you know when we reach your area.
