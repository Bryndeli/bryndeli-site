## Bryndeli Match: carers {#carers}

**What we collect**

When you fill in our carer sign up form, we collect:

- the first part of your postcode (for example TF4)
- the kinds of care work you do, your experience, when you're available, the hours you'd like and the rate you'd expect
- whether you'd work privately or self employed
- whether you need visa sponsorship to take a new role (yes, no or prefer not to say). We ask only so we know which roles could suit you. We never ask for visa details or documents at this stage
- anything you choose to tell us about how you find work now
- where you heard about us
- if you ask us to contact you: your first name, email address and, if you give it, your phone number, plus a record of your consent

**Why we use it**

- To contact you about care roles near you that fit what you told us, if you asked us to
- To understand what carers are looking for, so we can build a better service. We look at answers together, not at individuals, for this

**Our lawful basis**

We rely on your consent to contact you about roles. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing [privacy email], and we'll stop contacting you and delete your contact details. We rely on our legitimate interests to look at answers together to improve our service.

**Checks before an introduction**

If a role fits and you want to be introduced, we'll check your ID, right to work and references, and confirm your DBS status. We'll explain exactly what that involves, and ask for your agreement, before we start. We don't collect any of this information through the sign up form.

**Who we share it with**

- We only share your details with a family, agency or care home when you've agreed to be introduced to them.
- We use other companies to run our forms and store information for us: Google (Google Forms and Google Sheets) and Airtable. They act on our instructions and can't use your information for their own purposes.
- We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

**How long we keep it**

We keep your details until you ask us to remove them, or for 12 months after we were last in touch with you, whichever comes first.

## Bryndeli Match: hiring {#hire}

This section covers anyone who asks us to find a carer through our request form. If you're a care provider, the care providers section below also applies to you.

**What we collect**

When you ask us to find a carer, we collect:

- whether you're hiring as a family or individual, or as an agency or care home
- the first part of your postcode
- the help needed and when, in your own words
- your email address, and a record of your consent to be contacted
- which website or advert brought you to us

Please describe the tasks and hours only. Don't include names, addresses or medical details about the person who needs care. We don't need them to find you a carer.

**Why we use it**

- To find carers who might fit, and to contact you with a shortlist
- To follow up about your request

**Our lawful basis**

We use your information to take the steps you've asked us to take before any agreement between us, and we rely on your consent to contact you. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing [privacy email].

**Who we share it with**

- We may share a short summary of the help needed and your general area with carers we think could fit, so they can say whether they're interested. We don't share your email or other contact details with a carer until you've chosen to be introduced.
- We use Airtable to store requests for us, and [hosting provider] to run our website. They act on our instructions only.
- We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

**How long we keep it**

We keep your request for 12 months after we were last in touch about it, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. If you hire a carer through us, we keep a record of the introduction for [period agreed with your accountant] for accounting purposes.

## Bryndeli Match: care providers {#providers}

This section is for home care agencies, care homes, staffing agencies and other organisations that recruit carers through Bryndeli Match, and for the people who use Match on their behalf.

**What we collect**

- About the organisation: its name, type, address, Companies House number, CQC registration details and whether it holds a sponsor licence.
- About people at the organisation: name, job title, work email, work phone number and their role on the account (owner, recruiter or viewer).
- The roles the organisation wants to fill: tasks, hours, location, pay rate, start date and whether sponsorship is offered. Please don't include service users' names or medical details.
- Records of introductions, the terms accepted and invoices.

**Checks on public registers**

Before an organisation can see any carer's details, we check it against public registers: Companies House, the CQC register and, if it says it can sponsor visas, the Home Office register of licensed sponsors. We keep a record of what we checked and when.

**Why we use it**

- To verify that organisations are genuine, which protects the carers who use Match
- To find and introduce carers for the roles you've asked us to fill
- To manage your account, the terms you've agreed to, and invoices

**Our lawful basis**

We use this information to provide the service your organisation has asked for and to perform our agreement with it. We rely on our legitimate interests to verify organisations and protect carers, and to keep business records. Where we send marketing to individuals, we rely on consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

**Who we share it with**

- When a carer agrees to an introduction, we share the organisation's name, the role details and the contact details of the recruiter handling it.
- We use [hosting provider], Airtable, [email provider] and, once payments start, Stripe, to run the service for us. They act on our instructions only.
- We never sell your information.

**How long we keep it**

We keep account and contact details while the organisation uses Match and for 12 months after its last activity. We keep introduction records, agreements and invoices for [period agreed with your accountant] for legal and accounting purposes.

## Bryndeli Circle {#circle}

**What we collect**

If you join the Bryndeli Circle early access list, we collect your name, email address, who you organise care for, whether you employ PAs or private carers, anything you choose to write in the optional box, which website or advert brought you to us, and a record of your consent.

If you complete our survey about organising care at home, we collect your answers. The survey is anonymous unless you choose to leave your email, for early access, for the voucher draw or both.

Please don't include names, addresses or medical details about anyone in the free text boxes.

**Why we use it**

- To contact you about early access and founding places, if you asked us to
- To run the voucher draw, if you entered it: we use your email only to pick and contact winners
- To understand how families organise care, so we can decide what to build

**Our lawful basis**

We rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by emailing us or using the unsubscribe link in any email.

**Who we share it with**

Only the services that run our website, forms and emails, under contracts that protect your information: [hosting provider], Airtable, Google (Google Forms and Google Sheets) and [email provider]. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

**How long we keep it**

- Early access list: until you leave the list, or 12 months after early access opens, whichever comes first.
- Survey: answers are anonymised, and any emails left in the survey are deleted by [date].
- Voucher draw: entrant emails are deleted once the winners have been contacted.
