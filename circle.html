The problem

You shouldn't have to be the only one who knows.

You're organising care around a job, a family and a group chat that never stops. A new tablet from the GP, a fall on Tuesday, a carer who's never met Dad before. The important things live in your head, a notebook by the kettle and 300 WhatsApp messages. When something gets missed, you're the one who finds out.

How it works

Set it up once. Everyone stays current.

1

Add the care plan

Upload what you already have, whether that's a care plan, GP letters or your own notes. Bryndeli organises it.

2

Invite the team

Siblings, PAs, private carers and agency staff each get their own login on their phone. There's no app store download.

3

Everyone stays current

Carers ask questions like “Can Dad have ibuprofen?” and get answers from his plan. They see what changed since their last visit and leave a short note for the next person.
